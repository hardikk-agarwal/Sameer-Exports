# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Buyers at hardware importers, wholesalers, distributors and brands (buyers in the UK, US, Mexico, Germany, Canada and Australia), comparing Indian suppliers at a desk during work hours. They need to see range, finishes and capability quickly, then send item codes and quantities for a quote. Many arrive on old brass-builders.com catalogue links from search.

## Product Purpose
Website for Sameer Exports (Brass Builders), Aligarh: a manufacturer and exporter of solid brass and black iron builders' hardware since 1994. Success is a qualified quote request (form, email or WhatsApp) and a request for the full photo catalogue.

## Brand Personality
Precise, solid, export-ready. Plain factual voice, no superlatives. Confidence comes from real products, real item codes and real facts (est. 1994, ISO 9001, in-house sand casting and forging, Talanagri factory).

## Anti-references
The old table-and-GIF exporter site; generic Indian B2B directory listings; beige-and-gold "luxury" templates; dark "foundry fire" sites; AI-template landing pages (eyebrows on every section, three identical cards, stat tiles).

## Design Principles
1. Photography leads: large photographs of brass on doors, windows and cabinets, and of the factory, carry the page. Licensed stand-ins until the factory's own shoot.
2. Show, don't claim: real pieces with item codes instead of adjectives.
3. Every path ends in a quote: item codes, quantities, finish, WhatsApp.
4. Keep old links alive: legacy URLs redirect to the matching range.
5. Quiet interface: off-white page, one deep indigo accent, square buttons, no decoration the photographs don't need.

## Visual References
Dornbracht, VOLA, Vipp, Formani, deVOL, Buster + Punch, Corston, Joseph Giles: photographs covering most of the first screen, products shown in place or in close-up, calm light-weight type, square buttons, very little interface. Type is Archivo in two widths: wide for headings (like a cast maker's plate), normal for reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible focus, labels above inputs, inline errors, reduced-motion support, no hover-only information (touch devices get links and captions).
