## What changed from the original

- Clean vector curves (45 for the red face) instead of a 278 x 400 px picture, so edges stay crisp in print and on large screens.
- The dark outline and 3D edge are rebuilt as one even shape: 6 units wide all round, extruded 6 right and 7 down, the same as the original but without wobbles.
- The white fringe around the old cut-out is gone, so the mark sits cleanly on dark backgrounds.
- Colours fixed to two values. The shapes match the original to 99% (overlap of the red face 99.3%, whole silhouette 99.0%).

## Colours

- Red `#DA291C` (Pantone 485 C; RGB 218 41 28). The original's red measured RGB 216 37 28.
- Dark `#211916` (RGB 33 25 22), the original's outline colour. Use Pantone Black C in spot-colour print.

## Use

- Below about 48 px tall on screen (about 12 mm in print) use the flat mark: the outline and 3D edge get too thin to read.
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least half its width.
- Do not recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects. On photos, use the white version.
